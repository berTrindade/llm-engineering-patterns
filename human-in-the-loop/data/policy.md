# Tallow & Finch expense and refund policy (v1)

This policy is invented for this proof of concept. Tallow & Finch is not a real company.

## Expense claims

Categories and the maximum amount allowed per claim:

| Category | Limit per claim |
|---|---|
| travel | £500 |
| meals | £75 |
| office_supplies | £150 |
| client_entertainment | £200 |
| software | £300 |

Rules:

- A receipt is required for any expense over £25. A claim over £25 with no receipt must be
  rejected.
- A claim within its category limit, with a receipt where required, should be approved for the
  full claimed amount.
- A claim over its category limit should be a partial refund up to the category limit, unless it
  is missing a required receipt, in which case it is rejected instead.

## Refund requests

- The refund window is 30 days from the purchase date for standard-price items, and 14 days from
  the purchase date for items bought in a sale.
- A refund requested after the window has closed must be rejected.
- A refund amount can never exceed the original purchase price. A request for more than the
  original price should be a partial refund capped at that price.
- A refund within the window and at or under the original price should be approved for the
  amount requested.
