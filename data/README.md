# Data provenance

Everything in `office.json` is invented for this project: Harbour Street Studio, its 5 meeting
rooms, its 12 people and their working hours, and the existing meetings on the calendar. None of
it refers to a real company or person. The `now` field is a fixed timestamp, not the real clock,
so eval runs are reproducible.

`evals/tasks.jsonl` (30 scripted scheduling requests) is likewise invented for this project.
