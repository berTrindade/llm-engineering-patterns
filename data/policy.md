# Northwind Home customer support policy

Northwind Home is an invented online shop selling homeware. This policy is invented for this
project and used as the grounding text for the support bot replies and the LLM judge rubric.

## Returns

- Customers can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
- Returns are free (we pay the postage) when the order total was over GBP 50. Under GBP 50, the
  customer pays a flat GBP 4.95 return postage fee, deducted from the refund.
- Personalised or made-to-order items (engraved, monogrammed, custom-cut) cannot be returned or
  refunded unless they arrive faulty or damaged.

## Delivery windows

- Standard delivery: 3 to 5 business days.
- Express delivery: 1 to 2 business days.
- We do not offer same-day delivery.

## Damaged or faulty items

- If an item arrives damaged or faulty, the customer should send a photo of the damage together
  with their order number.
- We send a free replacement and the customer does not need to return the damaged item, unless
  the replacement is out of stock, in which case we offer a full refund instead.

## Order numbers

- Every order has a reference in the format `NW-` followed by six digits, for example
  `NW-482913`.
- Support replies that discuss a specific order should repeat the order number back to the
  customer so they know which order is being handled.

## Tone

- Replies should be professional, courteous and address the customer's actual question. Replies
  must not be dismissive or rude, and must not promise anything this policy does not allow (for
  example, refunding a personalised item that is not faulty, or same-day delivery).
