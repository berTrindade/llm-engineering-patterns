# Fernway Travel: customer support policy

Fernway Travel is an invented airline used only for this proof of concept. Every fact below
is invented for this repository and does not describe any real company's policy.

## Fare types

Fernway sells three fare types: Basic, Standard and Flex.

## Baggage allowances

| Fare type | Cabin bag | Checked bags included |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | 1 small bag (40 x 20 x 25 cm) | 0 |
| Standard | 1 cabin bag (55 x 40 x 23 cm) | 1 bag up to 23 kg |
| Flex | 1 cabin bag (55 x 40 x 23 cm) | 2 bags up to 23 kg each |

An extra checked bag can be added to any fare for £35 per bag, booked in advance. A bag that
exceeds 23 kg is charged a £20 overweight fee per bag, up to a maximum of 32 kg.

## Cancellation fees

| Fare type | Cancellation fee |
|---|---|
| Basic | Non-refundable, no cancellation allowed |
| Standard | £75 cancellation fee, remainder refunded as travel credit |
| Flex | £25 cancellation fee, remainder refunded to the original payment method |

Flex fares cancelled more than 14 days before departure have the £25 fee waived.

## Change fees

| Fare type | Change fee |
|---|---|
| Basic | Changes not permitted |
| Standard | £45 change fee plus any fare difference |
| Flex | No change fee, fare difference only |

A name change on any fare costs £30 and must be requested at least 48 hours before departure.
Fernway does not permit a name change within 48 hours of departure under any fare type.

## Refund timelines

- Refunds to the original payment method are processed within 7 business days of approval.
- Travel credit refunds are issued within 24 hours of approval and are valid for 12 months.
- A refund request must be submitted within 28 days of the cancelled flight's original
  departure date, or the right to a refund lapses.

## Seat selection

- Standard and Flex fares include free seat selection at booking.
- Basic fare passengers can select a seat for £12 for a standard seat or £25 for an
  extra-legroom seat.
- Seat selection can be changed up to 3 hours before departure, subject to availability.

## Policy exceptions

- Passengers with a documented medical need are exempt from the Basic fare's cabin bag size
  limit and may bring 1 additional small medical bag free of charge.
- A flight cancelled by Fernway entitles the passenger to a full refund to the original
  payment method regardless of fare type, with no cancellation fee, processed within
  7 business days.
- A Standard fare passenger who is denied boarding due to Fernway overbooking receives a
  full refund plus a £150 travel credit as compensation.
